Crazy Hunter - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information is processed when you play Crazy Hunter (the "Game", Android package com.crazyhunter.game), published by CNC TECH ("we", "us").

In short:

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-in and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not run any server of our own and we do not collect or receive personal data ourselves.
  • Your best score and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's advertising software inside the Game collects certain device data, described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The Game saves a small amount of data in the app's local storage on your device: your best score, your settings (such as sound), and whether the first-game tutorial hint has already been shown. This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. The Game reads your device's language setting only to display itself in your language; this is not stored or sent by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game displays full-screen ads between some games and optional rewarded ads (ads you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. The Game includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK. 3. Consent and legal bases (EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your choice through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. There you can consent to, or refuse, the storage of and access to information on your device and the use of your data for personalised advertising. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised on the basis of your profile; they may be selected using general information such as the app you are using and your approximate location.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising and for storing or accessing information on your device is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time as described in section 5. Displaying non-personalised ads and preventing fraud is based on our and Google's legitimate interest in financing and securing a free game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), to the extent permitted by law.

4. Data sharing, transfers and retention

  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share data with anyone other than through the Google advertising service described above.
  • Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google states that it relies on legal mechanisms such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses for such transfers.
  • We do not hold any personal data about you, so we have nothing to retain. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. Retention of data collected by Google is governed by Google's policies.
  • Data sent by Google's advertising software is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

5. Your choices and how to withdraw consent

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: on your Android device open Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices Settings → Privacy → Ads) and choose Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID. After deletion, apps can no longer use the ID to show you personalised ads.
  • Turn off ad personalisation by Google: visit My Ad Center or adssettings.google.com.
  • Change or withdraw the consent you gave in the Game: where a consent request applies to you, tap the Privacy button at the top of the game screen (the Game is shown in your device's language, or in English if that language is not supported, and the button's label follows that language, e.g. “Gizlilik” in Turkish) to reopen the consent options at any time. Alternatively, clear the Game's data (Settings → Apps → Crazy Hunter → Storage → Clear storage) or reinstall the Game; the consent form will be shown again the next time you open it in a region where it is required. Clearing the data also deletes your saved best score. Deleting your advertising ID (see above) also stops personalised ads.
  • Play without ads being loaded: the Game works offline. Without an internet connection no ads are requested and no data is sent.
  • Delete local data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the Game.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority. Because we do not hold data that identifies you, requests concerning advertising data are normally best addressed to Google using the tools linked above; you can also contact us and we will help as far as we can.

7. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country, for example 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal data through the Game, please contact us so that we can take appropriate steps. Parents can also delete the advertising ID on the child's device as described in section 5.

8. Security

We do not operate servers that store your data. Data kept on your device is protected by the security features of the Android operating system.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the Game gets new features or the law changes. The current version is always available on this page, with the date of the last update at the top. If a change is significant we will make it visible in the Game's store listing or in the Game.
